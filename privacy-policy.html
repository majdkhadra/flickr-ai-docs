When you create or access an account, we collect information needed to authenticate you, such as your email address, user ID, and login information from sign-in providers such as Google or Apple.

If you buy credits or subscriptions, purchases are processed through Apple. We receive information needed to verify purchases and manage your account, such as product identifiers, transaction confirmation data, subscription status, and credit balances.

Device, App, and Usage Information

We may collect technical and usage information, such as device type, operating system, app version, IP address, approximate location based on IP address, app interactions, generation counts, credit usage, crash data, diagnostics, and server logs.

Camera, Photo Library, and Notifications

With your permission, Flickr AI may access your camera, photo library, or notification settings. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

Support and Communications

If you contact us, we collect the information you provide, including your email address, message, attachments, and account details needed to respond.

Advertising and Marketing Information

We may collect or receive information related to ads, ad performance, marketing campaigns, or promotional communications. This may include device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and usage information, depending on the advertising or measurement services used.

3. AI Processing, Photos, and Face Data

Flickr AI uses Google AI models, including Gemini, Nano Banana, and other Google image generation or editing models, to provide AI image features.

When you request an AI generation or edit, your prompt, selected template information, and, if applicable, your uploaded photo are sent to our backend and may be transmitted to Google or related Google AI services for processing.

Photos may include faces. Flickr AI does not use uploaded photos to identify a person, authenticate identity, perform facial recognition, create faceprints, or extract facial geometry for biometric identification. We process uploaded photos only to provide the image generation or editing feature requested by the user.

We do not use your uploaded photos to train our own AI models.

Google and other service providers may process, temporarily store, cache, log, or retain prompts, uploaded files, outputs, and related technical information according to their own terms, data processing terms, security policies, and legal obligations. We do not control every technical retention period used by third-party AI or infrastructure providers.

4. Photo and Generation Storage

We do not intentionally store original uploaded source photos after they are sent for generation or editing.

We do store generated images, prompt history, timestamps, and related generation metadata so you can view your creation history in the app. At this time, Flickr AI does not support deleting individual creations from inside the app. You may delete your account or contact us to request deletion of account-associated data.
